Our story

Made for modern Indian homes.

Jinendra Kalakriti is a small studio in Alwar, Rajasthan. We make and curate décor that carries a little story: a toran that welcomes guests, a board for the entrance, a rangoli for Diwali, a keepsake to gift.

We like colour, craft and details that feel intentional. Every piece is photographed as it is, so what you see is what you receive.
